## The misconception
- Testing is often measured by test cases executed.
- Execution is the visible part, but not the valuable part.

## Where the real work happens
- Understanding the product and its users.
- Identifying risks before writing a single step.
- Choosing what *not* to test.

## Reading the result
- A pass does not prove quality; it proves one path worked once.
- Investigating failures: product bug, test bug, or environment?

## Takeaway
- Running the test is the easy part. Thinking is the job.
