## What AI is already good at in QA
- Drafting test scenarios from requirements.
- Summarising logs and failures.

## Where it falls short
- Product context, risk judgement, hallucinated assumptions.

## The new QA skill set
- Writing good prompts, reviewing AI output critically.

## Takeaway
- AI raises the floor. QA engineers still set the bar.
